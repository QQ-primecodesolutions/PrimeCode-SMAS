### Instagram caption

Not every business needs a website. Some do fine on word of mouth and a busy WhatsApp.

But here is how to tell if yours is losing something without one.

1. Customers ask you the same questions every day — your hours, your prices, where you are
2. People cannot find you when they search online
3. Your competitors look more established than you, even when your work is better
4. You are sending photos of your price list on WhatsApp
5. Someone asked for your website and you had to say you do not have one

If you ticked three or more, a website is probably costing you customers by not existing.

It does not need to be complicated or expensive. It needs to answer the questions your customers are already asking you.

Is your business in this position? Tell us in the comments.

**CTA:** Is your business in this position? Tell us in the comments.

**Hashtags:** #SmallBusiness #BusinessTechnology #Qwaqwa #PrimeCodeSolutions

### Visual brief

Tick-mark iconography, one sign per slide. Warm and helpful in tone — this post must never read as if it is mocking businesses that don't have a website. The final slide carries the soft conclusion, not a hard sell.

### Instagram caption

A school has a lot to communicate. Programmes, admissions, achievements, news, contact details — and parents asking about all of it.

Thiboloha Special School needed one place where all of that lives, and where a parent can find an answer without phoning the office.

**What we built:** a structured, responsive website covering programmes, admissions, news, achievements and contact information, organised so a parent can find what they need in a few taps, on a phone, on data.
