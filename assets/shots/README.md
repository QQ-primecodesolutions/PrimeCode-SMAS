# Screenshots for PROJECT-01 posts

Real screenshots of client sites, shown in drawn device frames. Only add a client's screenshots
after they have given written permission (recorded as `clientPermission` in the post file).

- **Format:** PNG or JPEG, under 8 MB.
- **Phone:** a portrait capture, for example 1080×2340, or from Chrome DevTools at 390×844 with
  3× pixel ratio. Portrait files are drawn in a phone.
- **Desktop:** a landscape capture, for example 1920×1200 (16:10) or 1920×1080. Landscape files
  are drawn in a laptop.
- **Cropping:** the top of the page is what shows. The image is scaled to the screen's width and
  cut off at the bottom, so capture the part that matters at the top.
- **Names:** `<client>-<page>-<phone|desktop>.png`, matching the `asset` paths in the post files.
